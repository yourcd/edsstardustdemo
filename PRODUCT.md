# PRODUCT — India Uncharted (redesign target)

_Provenance: stardust:direct (uplift), Mode A brand-faithful, from stardust/current + direction.md._

## Register
`brand` — inspirational lead-generation site for a boutique custom-India tour operator.

## Users
International and domestic leisure travelers planning a trip to India — heritage/Rajasthan tourists, wildlife-tour seekers, yoga & wellness travelers, honeymooners, families, motorcycle adventurers.

## Product Purpose
Inspire travelers, establish trust and craft, and convert to an enquiry/booking for curated & customizable India tour packages, transfers, and activities.

## Brand Personality
Warm, aspirational, hospitality-led, personal, editorial-boutique. "We don't just organize trips — we create stories you'll cherish for a lifetime." NOT a discount OTA.

## Anti-references
Cold corporate SaaS; generic blue-CTA OTA templates; cluttered discount aggregators; bargain "HOT DEALS" banners.

## Design Principles (target)
1. Photography fills the frame — it is the product.
2. One warm-earth system, coral-led, disciplined.
3. Elegant Tenor Sans display over friendly Poppins body, with a real scale.
4. Trust made tangible: testimonials, local expertise, 24/7 support.
5. Committed boutique voice — no bargain register.
