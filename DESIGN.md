# DESIGN — India Uncharted (redesign target, shared / Mode A)

Warm-earth boutique-travel system. Coral `#e3876e` is the single accent for CTAs and highlights; clay `#897055` and espresso `#3e2e1d` carry headings and dark bands; sand `#e5e3dc` and `#f8f8f8` are the warm neutrals; charcoal `#23282d` grounds the footer. The leaked pure-orange and blue shadow tint are retired.

Display type is **Tenor Sans**; body is **Poppins** with a real modular scale (base 17px, ~1.28 ratio) replacing the ad-hoc jumps. One radius ladder: 8px (chips/inputs), 16px (cards/media), 999px (pill CTAs). Soft single shadow `0 8px 24px rgba(62,46,29,.12)`.

Photography is restored to editorial scale (3:2 / 4:3, full-bleed heroes) with lower-band title overlays. Section rhythm is asymmetric — full-bleed image bands alternate with tighter editorial two-column sections — instead of a monotone centered stack.

Per-variant files: DESIGN-A (faithful+fixes), DESIGN-B (Tenor Sans amplified), DESIGN-C (photography + editorial motion). Only DESIGN-C carries `extensions.motion.register`.
