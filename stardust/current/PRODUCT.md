# PRODUCT — India Uncharted (current state, descriptive)

_Provenance: derived by stardust:extract from https://indiauncharted.com/ (single-page). Inferred sections marked._

## Register
`brand` — a marketing/lead-generation site for a travel agency. The primary job is to inspire, build trust, and capture an enquiry.

## Users
- Prospective leisure travelers to India (international and domestic).
- Segments visible in the IA: Rajasthan/heritage tourists, wildlife-tour seekers, yoga & wellness travelers, honeymooners, families, motorcycle-adventure riders.
- _provenance: inferred_ — basis: destination themes, package names, and meta description.

## Product Purpose
India Uncharted is a full-service, custom India tour operator. It sells curated and customizable tour packages (e.g. Rajasthan Wildlife, Goa Yoga & Wellness, Golden Triangle) plus transfers and activities, and drives visitors to enquire/book via phone or form.

## Brand Personality
Warm, aspirational, hospitality-led, and personal. Copy leans romantic and reassuring: "your trusted partner", "we create stories you'll cherish for a lifetime", "crafted journeys, unforgettable memories". The visual world is warm and earthy (coral, clay, sand, espresso) with elegant Tenor Sans display type — an editorial-boutique register rather than a discount-OTA one, though the "UNLIMITED CHOICES | BEST PRICES | HOT DEALS" strip pulls toward bargain-travel.

## Anti-references
- Cold, corporate SaaS aesthetics.
- Generic blue-CTA OTA templates.
- Cluttered discount-travel aggregators.

## Design Principles (observed)
- Photography-forward, but currently constrained.
- Warm earth palette anchored by a coral/terracotta accent.
- Elegant display type (Tenor Sans) over friendly body (Poppins).
- Trust signals: testimonials, star ratings, "happy clients".

## Tensions (see brand-review.html)
- Aspirational, editorial voice undercut by a bargain-travel banner and 2019-era template layout.
- Excellent destination photography rendered at thumbnail scale.
- Two competing type registers and an inconsistent radius/CTA ladder.
