Tensions surfaced

dated-patternTemplate-era hero + inline lead form

Centered hero over a single photo with a boxed multi-field capture form reads as a 2018–2019 WordPress travel template. The layout, double-CTA density, and form-in-hero pattern date an otherwise premium brand.

missed-opportunityExcellent photography shown at thumbnail scale

The captured imagery (Taj Mahal, Jaisalmer, Varanasi ghats, wildlife, desert boats) is genuinely strong, but the layout crops it into small cards and tiles. The brand's single biggest asset is never allowed to fill the frame.

ia-clutterVoice split between boutique and bargain

An elegant Tenor Sans / hospitality voice competes with a "UNLIMITED CHOICES | BEST PRICES | HOT DEALS" banner. The two registers pull the brand in opposite directions in the first viewport.

contrast-or-densityInconsistent radius & CTA ladder

Radii range across 5px, 6px, 10px, 20px, and 50px pills with no clear ladder; a stray pure-orange (#ff6600) and blue shadow tint leak in from theme defaults, muddying the warm-earth system.
